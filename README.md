# 🧟 Zombie Apocalypse

A simple browser-based zombie survival game made with **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**.

Survive increasingly difficult rounds, defeat different types of zombies, earn coins, purchase upgrades, and make it through the final boss!

## 🎮 How to Play

### Controls

* **W** — Move Up
* **A** — Move Left
* **S** — Move Down
* **D** — Move Right
* **Mouse** — Aim
* **Space** — Shoot

### Objective

Survive the zombie waves and reach **Round 35**.

Round 35 contains a special boss encounter. Defeat the boss to win the game.

If your health reaches zero, the game ends.

## 🧟 Enemies

There are several types of zombies:

### 🟦 Speedy Zombie

* Fast
* Low health
* Low damage
* Smaller than most other zombies

### 🟩 Normal Zombie

* Balanced speed and health
* The most common enemy

### 🟪 Strong Zombie

* High health
* Slow
* Deals more damage
* Drops more coins

### 🟥 Minion

* Appears during the final boss round
* Large
* Stronger than normal zombies
* Drops a large amount of coins

### 👹 Boss

The boss has **two stages**.

**Stage 1**

* 50 HP
* Large
* High damage

**Stage 2**

* 25 HP
* Smaller
* Faster
* Changes color

Defeating the boss completes the main game.

## 🪙 Coins & Upgrades

Defeating zombies gives you coins.

Coins can be spent between rounds in the upgrade shop.

Available upgrades:

* ❤️ **Health** — Increase maximum health
* 🔫 **Ammo** — Increase maximum ammunition
* 🏃 **Speed** — Increase movement speed

Upgrade prices increase as you purchase more levels.

## 🔄 Rounds

Each completed round increases the number of zombies in the next round.

The game becomes progressively more difficult.

**Round 35** is a special round containing:

* 1 Boss
* 2 Minions

After defeating the boss, you can choose to enter **Endless Mode**.

## ♾️ Endless Mode

Endless Mode allows you to continue fighting zombies after completing the main game.

Your health is restored when starting an endless round, and the game continues with a much larger zombie wave.

## 🧪 Developer Testing

The game includes a small developer testing cheat.

Press **P** during gameplay to give the player:

* 40 maximum health
* 100 maximum ammo
* Increased movement speed
* A later round number for testing

This is intended for development/testing rather than normal gameplay.

## 🎯 Project Goal

Zombie Apocalypse was created as a **JavaScript game-development learning project**.

The goal was to practice building a game from scratch while learning concepts such as:

* Game loops
* Canvas rendering
* Keyboard input
* Mouse input
* Collision detection
* Arrays and objects
* Enemy movement
* Randomized enemy spawning
* Bullets and projectiles
* Health and damage systems
* Game states
* Shops and upgrades
* Boss mechanics
* Round progression
